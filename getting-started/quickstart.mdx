From sign-up to your first scheduled post in a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.trypost.it/register](https://app.trypost.it/register) with your email, Google or GitHub. TryPost creates your first workspace for you — one workspace per brand or client, each with its own channels, members and settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about you">
    The welcome steps ask two quick questions:

    - **About you** — what best describes you (content creator, freelancer, agency, small business, …)
    - **Your goals** — what you want from TryPost (post everywhere at once, plan on a calendar, post via MCP, …)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan">
    Pick **Socials** (one workspace, $19/month) or **Workspaces** (unlimited workspaces, $99/month). Both include every feature and network, and you can switch later in [Plans and billing](/knowledge-base/settings/plans-and-billing). Checkout runs on Stripe and starts an **8-day free trial**; a card is required.

    <Note>
      Invited to someone else's account? You skip the plan step. If the owner has not subscribed yet, you see **Waiting for the account owner** until they do.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your first channel">
    After checkout you land in **Publish**. Click **New** > **Connect a new channel** in the sidebar (the **Publish** list of an empty workspace also shows **Welcome to TryPost 👋** with a **Connect channel** button), pick a network and authorize TryPost. On the confirmation page, tick the accounts you want and click **Finish connection**.

    TryPost opens the new channel's **Publish** page and asks **How many times a week would you like to post?** Pick a goal and TryPost adds matching posting times to the channel's queue. See [Connecting channels](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting) and the [platform guides](/platforms/linkedin) for each network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your first post">
    Click **New** > **Post** to open the composer. Pick the channels, write once, and add images or video from your computer or **More media sources**. Use **Customize for each network** when one network needs its own text or settings. See [Composer](/knowledge-base/publish/composer).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    Choose **Add to queue** to drop the post into the channel's next free posting time, set a date and time yourself, or publish now. Scheduled posts appear on the **Publish** page under **Queue**, and in the calendar. See [Queue](/knowledge-base/publish/queue).
  </Step>
</Steps>
