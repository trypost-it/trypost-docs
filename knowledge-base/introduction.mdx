## What is TryPost?

TryPost is an open-source social media scheduler. You write a post once, adapt it for each network, and publish or schedule it to LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram, Discord and Google Business from one place. Use it on [TryPost Cloud](https://app.trypost.it) or [host it yourself](/self-hosting/overview).

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.trypost.it/register](https://app.trypost.it/register) with your email, Google or GitHub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the welcome steps">
    **About you** → **Your goals** → **Plan**. The plan step takes you to Stripe Checkout. See [Plans and billing](/knowledge-base/settings/plans-and-billing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    **New** → **Connect a new channel**. See [Connecting a channel](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your first post">
    **New** → **Post** opens the [composer](/knowledge-base/publish/composer).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you were invited to an account whose owner has not subscribed yet, you see **Waiting for the account owner** until the subscription is active. The page updates by itself. Self-hosted installs have no plan step.
</Note>

## How the app is organized

The sidebar mirrors this Knowledge Base.

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Quick menu: **Post**, **Idea**, and, for the owner and admins, **Connect a new channel** and **Invite a team member**. |
| **Create** | [Ideas](/knowledge-base/create/ideas), [Templates](/knowledge-base/create/templates) and [Feeds](/knowledge-base/create/feeds): where content starts. |
| **Publish** | Your posts for all channels: [Queue, Approvals, Drafts and Sent](/knowledge-base/publish/overview), as a list or a calendar. |
| **Insights** | [Analytics](/knowledge-base/insights/overview) for the workspace and [for each channel](/knowledge-base/insights/channel-insights). |
| **Repurpose** (Beta) | [Republish](/knowledge-base/repurpose) every new Instagram or Facebook video to your other networks. Shown only to members who publish directly. |
| **Channels** | One entry per connected channel, each with its own **Publish** and **Insights** pages. |
| **Settings** | Personal (Profile, Preferences, Authentication, Notifications), Workspace (General, Channels, Members), Features (Signatures, Labels, Webhooks), Developers (API Keys, MCP) and Account (Billing). |

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) to open the command palette: "Search channels, pages and actions."

## Workspaces

A workspace is one brand or client. It has its own channels, posts, ideas, signatures, labels, analytics, members and MCP connection. Switch between workspaces from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar. See [Workspaces](/knowledge-base/settings/workspaces).

## Who can do what

There are no roles to pick. Each member of a workspace is either an **admin** or not, and either **Publishes directly** or **Needs approval**. The account owner is always an admin and always publishes directly.

- Everyone can write posts, ideas and templates.
- Publishing without review, approving other people's posts and Repurpose need **Publishes directly**.
- Channels, members, webhooks, API keys and workspace settings need an admin.

See [Members and permissions](/knowledge-base/settings/members-and-permissions) and [Approvals](/knowledge-base/publish/approvals).

## How publishing works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You save a post">
    Saving creates one post per selected channel. Each one can be edited, moved or deleted on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="TryPost publishes it on time">
    At its time (a custom time or a [queue slot](/knowledge-base/publish/queue)), TryPost sends the post to the network. Each channel is published on its own; if one fails, the others still go out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get an email">
    Notifications are sent by email. Choose which ones in [Notifications](/knowledge-base/settings/notifications).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## AI in TryPost

AI helps in three places, all in the web app:

- The composer's **AI Assistant** writes a post or rephrases, shortens, expands or changes the tone of your text. See [Composer](/knowledge-base/publish/composer#ai-assistant).
- **Generate ideas** on the [Ideas](/knowledge-base/create/ideas#generate-ideas) board.
- **Generate with AI** for image alt text in the [media editor](/knowledge-base/media#alt-text).

New text is written in your interface language. AI is not available through the REST API or the MCP server.
