---
title: "Queue"
description: "Give each channel posting times and let the queue fill them with posts."
---

The queue publishes posts at a channel's **posting times** (also called slots). Instead of picking a date for every post, you add it to the queue and TryPost places it in a free slot.

## Set up posting times

Each channel has its own posting times, in its own time zone.

- **The first time:** after you connect a channel, TryPost asks "How many times a week would you like to post?" Pick **Keep it steady** (1 time/week), **Build a presence** (3), **Reach new heights** (5) or **Choose your goal** (up to 28). TryPost then suggests recommended times you can **Customize**.
- **Any time later:** open the channel and choose **Manage posting times**, or go to its [Channel settings](/knowledge-base/channels/settings). There you set up to 4 times per day, turn days on or off, and generate times from your goal, from TryPost's recommendations, or by copying another channel.

A channel without posting times cannot use the queue: "Add posting times to this channel to use the queue."

## Add a post to the queue

In the [composer](/knowledge-base/publish/composer), choose:

| Option | Where the post goes |
| --- | --- |
| **Next available** | The first free slot. Gaps between queued posts are filled first. |
| **Prioritize** | The first slot not held by a post with a custom time. The queued posts right behind it, up to the next gap, each move one slot later. Nothing else moves. |

From **Drafts**, **Add to queue** puts a draft in the next free slot.

You can also click **+ New** on a free slot in the Queue list or the calendar. The composer opens at that slot's time; if you save without changing the time or the channel, the post takes exactly that slot.

## Posts keep their slot

A queued post stays in its slot. TryPost never moves it on its own.

- **Deleting**, **Move to Drafts**, publishing now or giving it a custom time frees the slot. The posts after it do **not** move up to fill the gap.
- **Next available** fills that gap the next time you queue a post.
- Any scheduled post on a slot's time holds that slot, even one scheduled with a custom time. The queue never puts two posts in the same slot.

## Reorder the queue

Drag a queued post onto another, or use **Move up**, **Move down** or **Move to top** from the post menu.

Reordering keeps the same set of times: the posts trade places among the times they already hold, and no new slot is taken.

## Posts waiting for approval

When a member who needs approval queues a post, the request holds its slot while it waits. The Queue shows it as a post card at that time:

- approvers see **Add to queue**, which approves it, and **Reject**;
- the member who asked can edit it;
- it cannot be dragged.

Approving keeps the post in that slot if the slot still exists and is free; otherwise it takes the first free slot. Rejecting or deleting the request frees the slot. Members who cannot see the request see a gap there, not **+ New**. See [Approvals](/knowledge-base/publish/approvals).

## When posting times or the time zone change

- **Changing posting times** moves only the posts whose slot no longer exists. They go to the first free slots, in order.
- **Changing the channel's time zone** first moves each queued post to the slot at the same local clock time in the new zone, then fills the first free slots in order. Posts with a custom time keep their moment.
- If no slot is left, a queued post keeps its time as a custom-time post.

## Show the slots

In the Publish filter menu, turn on **Show posting times** to see the free slots in the Queue list, each with **+ New**. **Manage posting times** opens the channel's settings.
