A repurpose watches one Instagram or Facebook account for new videos you post **outside** TryPost, and republishes each one to the other channels you pick. Post a Reel on Instagram, and the same video goes to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and the rest without copying it by hand.

**Repurpose** is in the sidebar, marked **Beta**. Only members who publish directly see it: the account owner, admins, and members whose **Publishing** access is **Publishes directly**. See [Members and permissions](/knowledge-base/settings/members-and-permissions).

## Create a repurpose

<Steps>
  <Step title="New repurpose">
    In **Repurpose**, click **New repurpose**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the source account">
    Choose the Instagram or Facebook account TryPost should watch, then click **Create**. Only these networks can be a source, "because they are the only networks that let us download the video."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure it">
    On the **Configuration** tab, choose what to watch for, the publishing mode and the destinations. Changes save as you make them (**Saved**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Click **Activate** at the top of the page. Only posts published after you activate are replicated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new repurpose starts as a **Draft**, so nothing is replicated until you activate it.

## Source

**Watch for** sets the kind of post that starts a repost: **Reels**, **Videos** or **Stories**.

An account can feed one repurpose per format. Creating a second one for the same account and format opens the existing one; changing an existing one to a taken pair fails with "This account already feeds another repurpose. Edit that one instead."

## Destinations

"Pick the accounts that receive it. Each one publishes in the format you choose."

- Any other connected channel can be a destination, except Google Business: "Google Business cannot be a destination: its posts do not accept video."
- For each destination you choose its post type, which must carry a video, and the same network settings as in the composer. A Story can become a TikTok video or a YouTube Short; the formats do not have to match.
- Settings a network needs to publish (the TikTok visibility, the Pinterest board, the Discord channel) must be filled in before you can activate or resume.
- The source account cannot also be a destination.

The caption of the original is reused. Only a caption longer than a network's limit is changed: it is shortened with AI, or cut at a word when AI is not available. Instagram destinations keep only the first 5 hashtags.

## Publishing

| Option | What happens when a new video is found |
| --- | --- |
| **Publish automatically** | "Each new post is scheduled the moment it is found." |
| **Create as draft** | "Each new post becomes a draft here for you to review and publish." |

Posts created by a repurpose appear in [Publish](/knowledge-base/publish/overview) like any other post.

<Note>
  Edge case: if the member who created the repurpose later loses direct publishing (their access changes to **Needs approval**), the posts it creates go to [Approvals](/knowledge-base/publish/approvals) instead of being scheduled.
</Note>

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Set up, not watching yet. |
| **Active** | TryPost checks the account regularly and replicates every new post. |
| **Paused** | Checks are on hold. **Resume** picks up where it stopped, so nothing posted meanwhile is lost. |
| **Disabled** | Turned off. **Activate** again starts fresh: whatever you posted while it was off is not replicated. |

The **Activate**, **Pause**, **Resume** and **Disable** buttons are at the top of the repurpose page. The Repurpose list shows each one's **Replicated** count and **Last checked** time.

On TryPost Cloud, an active repurpose checks its source about every 15 minutes. If the network limits requests, the next check waits longer.

<Note>
  Self-hosting TryPost? The check interval and back-off are set with `REPURPOSE_POLL_INTERVAL_MINUTES` and `REPURPOSE_BACKOFF_MINUTES`, and need the scheduler running. See [Configuration](/self-hosting/configuration).
</Note>

## When an account stops working

- **The source needs reconnecting:** the repurpose stops by itself and shows "Replication is on hold: the account this repurpose watches needs to be reconnected." [Reconnect](/knowledge-base/channels/settings) the account and the repurpose resumes on its own.
- **No destination is left:** it stops with "Replication is on hold: no destination is available. Add one, then resume it."
- **A destination needs reconnecting:** the repurpose keeps running. Posts for that channel are still created and fail visibly, so you can retry them after reconnecting.
- **The source was disconnected:** the repurpose has no source until you pick one. Disconnecting a channel reports how many repurposes were paused. See [Disconnecting a channel](/knowledge-base/channels/disconnecting).

When the problem is fixed, the banner says "The problem is fixed. Resume this repurpose to start replicating again." A repurpose that stopped by itself resumes from the moment you resume it, so the videos posted during the outage are not replayed. No email is sent when a repurpose stops.

## Activity

The **Activity** tab lists every source post TryPost has seen ("Posts this account publishes outside TryPost will show up here."), with its status and the posts it created.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Queued** | Waiting to be downloaded. |
| **Processing** | Downloading the video or creating the posts. |
| **Replicated** | Posts created and scheduled. |
| **Drafted** | Posts created as drafts. |
| **Skipped** | Not replicated on purpose. |
| **Failed** | Something went wrong. |

Skipped and failed items give the reason:

- Already published through TryPost: the original was posted from TryPost, so it is not copied back.
- The network did not share a downloadable file, usually because of copyrighted audio.
- The video could not be downloaded.
- Could not create the posts.
- No destination was available to publish to.

## Delete a repurpose

Open **More actions** at the top of the repurpose page and choose **Delete repurpose**: "Checks stop immediately. Posts already created stay in your calendar."
