Your profile belongs to you, not to a workspace: the same name, email and photo show in every workspace you are a member of. Open it from **Settings → Profile**.

## Profile photo

Under **Profile photo**, upload an image from your computer or remove the current one.

## Profile information

Under **Profile information**, change your **Name** and **Email address**, then click **Save**.

The email address is the one you sign in with and where TryPost sends your emails. After you change it, the new address is unverified: the page shows **Your email address is unverified.** with a link to resend the verification email. Open the link in that email to verify it.
