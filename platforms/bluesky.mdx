# Bluesky

TryPost publishes posts and threads to Bluesky.

## Connect your account

1. In the sidebar, open **Channels** and click **Connect channel** (or use **New** > **Connect a new channel**)
2. Choose **Bluesky** and click **Connect Bluesky**
3. On the **Connect Bluesky** form, enter your **Handle or email** and an **App Password**, then click **Connect Bluesky**
4. On the confirmation page, check the account and click **Finish connection**

TryPost opens the new channel's **Publish** page and asks how many times a week you want to post. See [Connecting channels](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting) for the full flow.

<Note>
  Bluesky doesn't use OAuth. Create an [App Password](https://bsky.app/settings/app-passwords) in your Bluesky settings — do not use your main password.
</Note>

## Supported content types

| Type | `content_type` | Description |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **Post** | `bluesky_post` | Text with up to 4 optional images **or** 1 video |

## Media limits

| `content_type` | Image | Video | Duration | Format |
|----------------|-------|-------|----------|--------|
| `bluesky_post` | 10 MB | 300 MB | 10 minutes | Images JPEG/PNG/GIF/WebP. Video MP4 or MOV |

The video size is the lexicon's decimal value (300 000 000 bytes). Bluesky's image blob limit is 2 MB (2 000 000 bytes), but TryPost re-encodes every non-GIF image under it at publish, so only the 10 MB hard cap applies to the original; a GIF over 2 MB fails at publish. Official specs: [`app.bsky.embed.video`](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/lexicons/app/bsky/embed/video.json), [`app.bsky.embed.images`](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/lexicons/app/bsky/embed/images.json); the 10-minute limit was [announced by Bluesky on 26 August 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/bluesky-now-lets-you-upload-10-minute-long-videos/). Full catalog: [Media](/knowledge-base/media#bluesky).

## Text and per-post options

- **300 characters** per post. TryPost counts code points, so an emoji counts as one.
- **Threads** — click **Start thread** to add replies under the post, up to **24**. Each reply has its own text and up to 4 media items, following the same rules as a post.
- **Link preview** — the first link gets a preview card. Remove it with the **×** on the card (`meta.link_preview: false` on the API).
- **Alt text** — up to 2,000 characters per image.

## Insights

Bluesky posts and followers appear in [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview).

## Self-hosting setup

No API credentials are required for Bluesky. It works out of the box on any self-hosted instance.

<Accordion title="Optional Bluesky settings">
  Accounts connect through the default PDS, `https://bsky.social`. The connect form has no field for another server; to point every Bluesky connection at a different PDS, set:

  ```env
  BLUESKY_DEFAULT_SERVICE=https://bsky.social
  ```

  Hide Bluesky with `BLUESKY_ENABLED=false`.
</Accordion>
