# Facebook

TryPost publishes posts, reels and stories to the Facebook pages you manage.

## Connect your account

1. In the sidebar, open **Channels** and click **Connect channel** (or use **New** > **Connect a new channel**)
2. Choose **Facebook** and click **Connect Facebook**
3. Authorize TryPost on Facebook and keep every permission checked
4. On the **Select accounts** page, tick the pages you want and click **Finish connection**

Every page you manage is listed, and you can connect several at once. Pages that are already connected show as **Connected**. TryPost then opens the new channel's **Publish** page and asks how many times a week you want to post. See [Connecting channels](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting) for the full flow.

## Supported content types

| Type | `content_type` | Aspect ratio | Description |
|------|----------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Post** | `facebook_post` | — | Text with up to 10 images or 1 video |
| **Reel** | `facebook_reel` | 9:16 | Short-form vertical video |
| **Story** | `facebook_story` | 9:16 | One image or video, visible for 24 hours. Stories carry no caption, so the post text is not sent |

The composer lets you mix images and a video on a Post, but Facebook gets only one kind: the video when it is the first file (the images are dropped), otherwise the images (the video is dropped).

## Media limits

| `content_type` | Image | Video | Duration | Format |
|----------------|-------|-------|----------|--------|
| `facebook_post` | 4 MB | 1 GB | 240 minutes | JPEG/PNG/WebP (no GIF). Video MP4 or MOV |
| `facebook_reel` | — | 1 GB | 90 seconds | MP4 recommended, 9:16 |
| `facebook_story` | 10 MB | 1 GB | 60 seconds | Images are fitted to 9:16. MP4 recommended |

Reel and Story videos must be vertical, close to 9:16 (width ÷ height between 0.5 and 0.6), or the post cannot be scheduled.

Images are published at their own aspect ratio; TryPost does not crop them on the server. A Story needs an image or a video. Official specs: [Reels](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/video-api/guides/reels-publishing/), [Stories](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/page-stories-api/), [Video errors](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/video-api/reference/error-codes/). Full catalog: [Media](/knowledge-base/media#facebook).

## Text and per-post options

- **10,000 characters** per post.
- **Link preview** — the first link in a post gets a preview card. Remove it with the **×** on the card (`platforms[].meta.link_preview: false` on the API), or use **Replace link preview with media**.
- **Alt text** — up to 1,000 characters per image.

## Insights

Facebook page metrics and followers appear in [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview).

A Facebook page can also be a [Repurpose](/knowledge-base/repurpose) source: TryPost watches it for new Reels, videos or Stories you publish outside TryPost and republishes them to the channels you pick.

## OAuth scopes

`public_profile`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_manage_posts`, `read_insights`, `business_management`

`read_insights` powers page and post metrics in Insights.

## Self-hosting setup

<Accordion title="Configure Facebook API credentials">
  1. Go to [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/) and create an app
  2. Add the **Facebook Login** product
  3. Set the redirect URL to `{APP_URL}/accounts/facebook/callback`
  4. Request permissions: `public_profile`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_manage_posts`, `read_insights`, `business_management`
  5. Add to your `.env`:

  ```env
  FACEBOOK_CLIENT_ID=your_app_id
  FACEBOOK_CLIENT_SECRET=your_app_secret
  FACEBOOK_CLIENT_REDIRECT="${APP_URL}/accounts/facebook/callback"
  ```

  The same app also serves [Instagram via Facebook](/platforms/instagram#self-hosting-setup).

  Hide Facebook with `FACEBOOK_ENABLED=false`.
</Accordion>
