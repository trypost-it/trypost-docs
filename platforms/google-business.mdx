# Google Business Profile

TryPost publishes posts to your Google Business Profile — updates, events and offers that appear on your Business Profile in Google Search and Maps. Each connected channel is one business **location**.

## Connect your account

1. In the sidebar, open **Channels** and click **Connect channel** (or use **New** > **Connect a new channel**)
2. Choose **Google Business Profile** and click **Connect Google Business Profile**
3. Sign in with the Google account that manages your business and allow every permission
4. On the **Select accounts** page, tick the locations you want (each card is marked **Location**) and click **Finish connection**

Every location of every Business account that login manages is listed, except locations Google does not allow to post. If none is found, TryPost shows *No Google Business Profile locations found. Verify your business first.* After you finish, TryPost opens the new channel's **Publish** page and asks how many times a week you want to post. See [Connecting channels](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting) for the full flow.

<Tip>
  Managing locations from more than one Google login? Use **Switch account** on the confirmation page: Google asks which account to use again.
</Tip>

## Supported content types

| Type | `content_type` | Description |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **Post** | `google_business_post` | Text with an optional image. Appears on your Business Profile in Search and Maps |

## Media limits

| `content_type` | Image | Video | Format |
|----------------|-------|-------|--------|
| `google_business_post` | 1 image, 10 MB | — | JPEG/PNG/WebP. At least 250 × 250 px. No GIF, no PDF |

TryPost converts the image to JPEG (up to 2048 px wide, under 5 MB) before sending it. **Edit Media** offers the crop presets 4:3 and 1:1. Full catalog: [Media](/knowledge-base/media#google-business-profile).

## Post types and options

**Text.** Up to **1,500 characters**, sent as plain text. The text and the image are each optional.

**Post type** (`platforms[].meta.topic_type`):

| Post type | `topic_type` | What it needs |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| **What's New** (default) | `STANDARD` | Nothing extra. News and updates about your business |
| **Event** | `EVENT` | **Event title** (up to 58 characters), **Start** and **End** dates. Optional start and end times, in the location's local time |
| **Offer** | `OFFER` | **Offer title** (up to 58 characters), **Start** and **End** dates. Optional coupon code, redeem link and terms (up to 5,000 characters). Google adds a **View offer** button |

On the API, the dates go in `meta.event` (`title`, `start_date`, `end_date` as `YYYY-MM-DD`, `start_time`, `end_time` as `HH:MM`) and the offer details in `meta.offer` (`coupon_code`, `redeem_online_url`, `terms_conditions`). The end date must be on or after the start date, and a same-day end time must be after the start time.

**Button** (`meta.call_to_action`, not available on offers):

| Button | `action_type` | Needs a link |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| **None** (default) | `NONE` | — |
| **Book** | `BOOK` | Yes |
| **Order online** | `ORDER` | Yes |
| **Buy** | `SHOP` | Yes |
| **Learn more** | `LEARN_MORE` | Yes |
| **Sign up** | `SIGN_UP` | Yes |
| **Call now** | `CALL` | No — Google uses the location's phone number |

The **Button link** (`meta.call_to_action.url`, http or https, up to 2,048 characters) is required for every button except **None** and **Call now**. An event or offer without its title and dates, or a button without its link, cannot be scheduled or published.

**Language.** Google receives the post in the language of the author's TryPost interface.

## Review by Google

Google reviews every post before it goes live. While it does, the post shows **In review by Google**; TryPost checks every 5 minutes and marks it published once Google puts it live.

- If Google rejects it, the post fails with *Google rejected this post in review. Edit the content or image and try again.* A rejected post cannot be retried — duplicate it, fix it and schedule the copy.
- If Google has not confirmed the post after 24 hours, it fails with *Google never confirmed this post. Check your Business Profile and try again.*

TryPost cannot edit or delete a post on Google after it is published. Deleting it in TryPost leaves it on your Business Profile.

## Insights

Google Business Profile is not included in [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview), and it cannot be a [Repurpose](/knowledge-base/repurpose) destination.

## OAuth scopes

`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile`, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email`, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage`

`business.manage` is the scope TryPost needs to publish.

## Self-hosting setup

<Accordion title="Configure Google Business Profile API credentials">
  Google Business Profile uses its **own** Google OAuth client, separate from the one for YouTube and Google login, so the sensitive `business.manage` scope never sends your YouTube app back into Google's review.

  1. In the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/), create or choose a project
  2. Request access to the Business Profile APIs. Google approves it manually and the API quota stays at 0 until it does; check Google's current eligibility rules (a verified, active Business Profile) before you apply
  3. Enable the **My Business Account Management API**, the **My Business Business Information API** and the Google My Business API (v4), which is the only API for Local Posts
  4. Configure the OAuth consent screen with the three scopes listed above
  5. Create OAuth 2.0 credentials with the redirect URL `{APP_URL}/accounts/google-business/callback`
  6. Add to your `.env`:

  ```env
  GOOGLE_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID=your_client_id
  GOOGLE_BUSINESS_CLIENT_SECRET=your_client_secret
  GOOGLE_BUSINESS_CLIENT_REDIRECT="${APP_URL}/accounts/google-business/callback"
  ```

  Hide Google Business Profile with `GOOGLE_BUSINESS_ENABLED=false`. The scheduler must be running for posts to leave **In review by Google** — see [Production setup](/self-hosting/production#scheduled-tasks-cron).
</Accordion>
