# Mastodon

TryPost publishes posts and threads to any Mastodon instance.

## Connect your account

1. In the sidebar, open **Channels** and click **Connect channel** (or use **New** > **Connect a new channel**)
2. Choose **Mastodon** and click **Connect Mastodon**
3. On the **Connect Mastodon** form, enter your **Instance URL** (for example `mastodon.social`) and click **Continue with Mastodon**
4. Authorize TryPost on your instance
5. On the confirmation page, check the account and click **Finish connection**

TryPost opens the new channel's **Publish** page and asks how many times a week you want to post. See [Connecting channels](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting) for the full flow.

## Supported content types

| Type | `content_type` | Description |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **Post** | `mastodon_post` | Text with up to 4 optional images or 1 video |

## Media limits

| `content_type` | Image | Video | Format |
|----------------|-------|-------|--------|
| `mastodon_post` | 10 MB | 40 MB | JPEG/PNG/GIF/WebP; video MP4 or MOV |

Official specs: [Attachments](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/user/posting/#attachments), [`Instance.media_attachments`](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/entities/Instance/). Full catalog: [Media](/knowledge-base/media#mastodon).

## Text and per-post options

- **500 characters** per post, **including the content warning**. TryPost uses the default Mastodon limit even if your instance allows more.
- **Content warning** — hide the post behind a warning (`platforms[].meta.spoiler_text`, up to 500 characters). In a thread, the warning is repeated on every reply.
- **Threads** — click **Start thread** to add replies under the post, up to **24**. Each reply has its own text and up to 4 media items.
- **Alt text** — up to 1,500 characters per image.

## Insights

Mastodon posts and followers appear in [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview).

## OAuth scopes

`read:accounts`, `read:statuses`, `write:statuses`, `write:media`

## Self-hosting setup

No API credentials are required for Mastodon. TryPost registers an OAuth app on each instance the first time someone connects from it.

<Accordion title="Optional Mastodon settings">
  Each account remembers the instance it connected from. `MASTODON_DEFAULT_INSTANCE` is only the fallback for an account that has no stored instance; you rarely need to change it:

  ```env
  MASTODON_DEFAULT_INSTANCE=https://mastodon.social
  ```

  Hide Mastodon with `MASTODON_ENABLED=false`.
</Accordion>
