# Threads

TryPost publishes posts and ghost posts to Threads.

## Connect your account

1. In the sidebar, open **Channels** and click **Connect channel** (or use **New** > **Connect a new channel**)
2. Choose **Threads** and click **Connect Threads**
3. Authorize TryPost on Threads
4. On the confirmation page, check the account and click **Finish connection**

TryPost opens the new channel's **Publish** page and asks how many times a week you want to post. See [Connecting channels](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting) for the full flow.

## Supported content types

| Type | `content_type` | Description |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **Thread** | `threads_post` | Text with up to 10 optional images **or** 1 video |
| **Ghost post** | `threads_ghost_post` | Text only, archived after 24 hours. No media and no topic |

## Media limits

| `content_type` | Image | Video | Duration | Format |
|----------------|-------|-------|----------|--------|
| `threads_post` | 8 MB | 1 GB | 5 minutes | JPEG/PNG, aspect ratio up to 10:1 either way; video MOV or MP4 |

**Edit Media** offers the crop presets 1:1, 4:5 and 1.91:1. Official specs: [Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/overview/), [Posts](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/posts/). Full catalog: [Media](/knowledge-base/media#threads).

## Text and per-post options

- **500 characters** per post.
- **Topic** — tag the post with a topic (`platforms[].meta.topic_tag`): 1 to 50 characters after a leading `#` is dropped, without `.` or `&`.
- **Link preview** — Threads always shows a preview card for the first link; it cannot be removed.
- **Alt text** — up to 1,000 characters per image.

## Insights

Threads posts and followers appear in [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview).

## OAuth scopes

`threads_basic`, `threads_content_publish`, `threads_manage_insights`

`threads_manage_insights` powers post and account metrics in Insights.

## Self-hosting setup

<Accordion title="Configure Threads API credentials">
  1. Go to [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/) and create an app
  2. Add the **Threads** product
  3. Request the scopes above in the app's permissions section
  4. Set the redirect URL to `{APP_URL}/accounts/threads/callback`
  5. Add to your `.env`:

  ```env
  THREADS_CLIENT_ID=your_app_id
  THREADS_CLIENT_SECRET=your_app_secret
  THREADS_CLIENT_REDIRECT="${APP_URL}/accounts/threads/callback"
  ```
</Accordion>
