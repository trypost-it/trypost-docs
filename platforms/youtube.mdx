# YouTube

TryPost uploads YouTube Shorts to your channels.

## Connect your account

1. In the sidebar, open **Channels** and click **Connect channel** (or use **New** > **Connect a new channel**)
2. Choose **YouTube** and click **Connect YouTube**
3. Pick your Google account on Google's account chooser and authorize TryPost
4. On the **Select accounts** page, tick the channels you want and click **Finish connection**

Every channel of that Google login is listed, and you can connect several at once. If the login has no YouTube channel, TryPost shows *No YouTube channels found. Please create a channel first.* After you finish, TryPost opens the new channel's **Publish** page and asks how many times a week you want to post. See [Connecting channels](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting) for the full flow.

## Supported content types

| Type | `content_type` | Aspect ratio | Description |
|------|----------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Short** | `youtube_short` | 9:16 | Vertical video up to 3 minutes (a video is required) |

## Media limits

| `content_type` | Video | Duration | Format |
|----------------|-------|----------|--------|
| `youtube_short` | 1 GB | 3 minutes | MP4 or MOV |

Official specs: [`videos.insert`](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert), [3-minute Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877). Full catalog: [Media](/knowledge-base/media#youtube).

## Title, description and settings

**Title.** Set it in the YouTube settings (**Title**, `meta.title`, up to 100 characters, no `<` or `>` — rejected even on drafts). Leave it empty and TryPost uses the first non-empty line of your post text, with `<` and `>` removed and cut to 100 characters. YouTube rejects an upload without a title, so a post with neither a title nor any text cannot be scheduled or published.

**Description.** The post text, unless you set **Description** (`meta.description`, plain text, at most 5,000 bytes).

The post text (your description when **Description** is empty) is capped at **5,000 characters**.

| Setting | `meta` key | Values |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| **Category** | `category_id` | `1` Film & Animation, `2` Autos & Vehicles, `10` Music, `15` Pets & Animals, `17` Sports, `19` Travel & Events, `20` Gaming, `22` People & Blogs (default), `23` Comedy, `24` Entertainment, `25` News & Politics, `26` Howto & Style, `27` Education, `28` Science & Technology, `29` Nonprofits & Activism |
| **Visibility** | `privacy_status` | `public` (default), `unlisted`, `private` |
| **License** | `license` | `youtube` (Standard YouTube License), `creativeCommon` (Creative Commons - Attribution) |
| **Notify subscribers** | `notify_subscribers` | boolean, default on |
| **Allow embedding** | `embeddable` | boolean, default on |
| **Made for kids** | `made_for_kids` | boolean, default off |
| **AI-generated** | `is_ai_generated` | boolean — discloses altered or synthetic content |

## Insights

YouTube videos and subscribers appear in [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview).

## OAuth scopes

`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload`, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly`, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl`, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly`

`yt-analytics.readonly` powers YouTube metrics in Insights.

## Self-hosting setup

<Accordion title="Configure Google/YouTube API credentials">
  1. Go to [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/) and create a project
  2. Enable the **YouTube Data API v3** and **YouTube Analytics API**
  3. Create OAuth 2.0 credentials
  4. Add the scopes listed above to the OAuth consent screen
  5. Set the redirect URL to `{APP_URL}/accounts/youtube/callback`
  6. Add to your `.env`:

  ```env
  GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=your_client_id
  GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET=your_client_secret
  GOOGLE_CLIENT_REDIRECT="${APP_URL}/accounts/youtube/callback"
  ```

  <Note>
    The same Google credentials are used for YouTube and [Google login](/self-hosting/configuration#google). Add both callback URLs to your OAuth client. Google Business Profile uses [its own Google app](/platforms/google-business#self-hosting-setup).
  </Note>

  Hide YouTube with `YOUTUBE_ENABLED=false`.
</Accordion>
